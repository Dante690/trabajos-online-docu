**Here you will find information about how our platform works, post job offers, answer questions and doubts, tips to improve your profile and job search or recruitment experience.** <img src="/img/logo-trabajos.jpg" alt="Trabajosonline Logo" title="Welcome to Trabajosonline" width="20" height="20" />

## What is Trabajosonline?

:briefcase: **Trabajosonline** is a job portal where you can find job offers for salaried employees and freelancers in the IT, Fintech, Web3, and Marketing technology sectors. :computer:

- **Technology and Engineering**: Fullstack, Frontend, Backend, Crypto, Developer, Engineer :computer:
- **Marketing and Finance**: Marketing, Finance :bar_chart:
- **Executive and Senior Roles**: Executive, Senior :briefcase:
- **And Others**: For other types of jobs that do not fit the above
