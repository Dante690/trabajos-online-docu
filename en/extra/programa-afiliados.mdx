## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign Up">
    Create your affiliate account and agree to the program terms. It takes about **5 minutes**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share">
    Get your unique referral link and share it with your audience.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Track">
    Monitor your referrals and commissions in real time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get Paid">
    Receive your commissions automatically each month.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### 1. Sign up for the program

Go to the [Affiliate Program](https://trabajosonline.org/en/affiliate-program) page and click **Start Earning** / **Sign up**. If you already have a Trabajosonline account, just log in.

![Affiliate program sign up](/logo/afiliados-signup.png "Affiliate sign up")

### 2. Get your code and your link

Once inside, go to **Partnerships** in the sidebar. There you'll find two tabs: **Affiliate Program** and **Referral Program**.

In the **Affiliate Program** tab you'll see:

- **Your Affiliate Code** — your affiliate code (for example `AFFBB5AEE`). Share it with your audience: they can enter it during registration to be linked to your affiliate account.
- **Your Referral Link** — your referral link, in the format `https://trabajosonline.org/register?aff=YOUR_CODE`. Share it on social media, blogs or newsletters: anyone who signs up through it is tracked automatically.

![Partnerships dashboard with the affiliate code](/logo/afiliados-partnerships.png "Partnerships dashboard")

### 3. Track your results

The same dashboard gives you real-time metrics:

| Metric | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Total Earnings** | All-time earnings |
| **Referrals** | Total referred clients |
| **Pending** | Amount awaiting payout |

<Tip>
  Use the **Copy** button next to the code and the link to copy them without transcription errors.
</Tip>

## Program terms

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Referral window">
    Referrals are tracked for **30 days** from the initial click using **last-click attribution**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Action locking">
    Actions are locked **1 month and 3 days** after the end of the month they are tracked.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Invoicing">
    Actions are invoiced on the **5th of the month** after they lock.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Payout scheduling">
    Approved transactions are paid **30 days** after the end of the day they are invoiced.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Registration">
    **Free to join** — no registration fees required.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Note>
  Several weeks pass between a commission being generated and you getting paid, due to the lock → invoice → payout cycle. That's how the program works, not a delay.
</Note>
