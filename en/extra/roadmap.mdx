## Betting on a future with more opportunities

At Trabajosonline our vision focuses on expanding hiring possibilities, both for freelancers and salaried employees, offering flexible payments in fiat currencies (€, $) and cryptocurrencies. Our mission is to develop our marketplace for freelancers and companies, creating an environment where innovation and opportunity are within everyone's reach.

The market is evolving towards **intent-based job hunting**, powered by AI agents that detect real hiring signals and social interaction patterns across platforms. That's what we're building 🚀.
