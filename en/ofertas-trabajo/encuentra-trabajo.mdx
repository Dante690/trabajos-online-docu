## Find Jobs 💼

At Trabajos Online, we strive for excellence in the quality of job offers published for our users. To achieve this, we apply quality filters using our JobRank algorithm 💻 to discard job offers that do not meet minimum requirements or do not align with our page 🚫.

## Salaried Job Offers 💸

You can search for job offers through [our platform](https://trabajosonline.org).
On our platform, you can filter jobs by language. By selecting a different language, such as Spanish 🇪🇸, English 🇺🇸, or German 🇩🇪, jobs available in that language are prioritized, appearing at the top of your search, ensuring that you find the most relevant opportunities easily.

![Example of job offers in Spanish](/logo/buscaempleo1.png "Job offers in Spanish")

If you want to conduct a more extensive search, you can use the advanced filters to customize your results and see only the opportunities that match your specific criteria 🕵️‍♂️. The filtering options available include:

![Searching for job offers using filters](/logo/buscaempleo2.png "Filters for searching job offers")

### Available Filtering Options

The filtering options available include:

- **Language**: Similar to what was mentioned above, you can search for offers in your preferred language 🌐.
- **Categories**: Filter by specific sectors or areas of interest 📂.
- **Minimum Salary**: Specify a minimum salary threshold, for example, over $30,000 💵.
- **Location**: Explore opportunities in any country in the world 🌍.
- **Urgency**: Find offers that need to be filled urgently, within less than a week ⏳.
- **Position Type**: lets you choose the kind of work arrangement that best fits your availability and career goals.
You can filter opportunities by Full-time roles for long-term employment, Part-time positions for more flexible schedules, or Contract / Freelance roles for project-based or independent work.
- **Hide Closed Offers**: Exclude from your search those positions no longer available ❌.
- **Tags**: Search for jobs using specific keywords, such as "JavaScript", "React", among others 🔖.

### Search for a Specific Position

Additionally, for those who have a particular position in mind, our search bar makes it easy to quickly locate offers that exactly match what you are looking for 🔍.

## Freelance Job Offers 💻

Our platform provides direct access to a variety of job offers, including those aimed at independent professionals or freelancers 💼. These specific opportunities are clearly marked with a green "Freelancer" label, making them easy to identify among the wide range of options available.

![How to identify job offers for Freelancers](/logo/buscaempleo3.png "Freelancer Job Offer")

To apply for these freelance jobs, it is an essential requirement that users are previously registered on our platform. Registration can be completed using an email address 📧 or through a digital wallet, thus offering flexibility and security in the application process.
