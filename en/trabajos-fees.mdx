## Transparent pricing

At TrabajosOnline.org we want you to know exactly what you'll pay. No hidden costs, no surprises 💎.

## 🏢 For companies

| Item | Price |
|---|---|
| **Job Posting — Basic Price** | **€60** |
| **Job Posting — For Freelancers** | **FREE** |

<Note>
  Posting a freelance job is **free**. The €60 fee applies only to salaried job offers published by companies.
</Note>

## 👥 For clients (agencies, contracts, companies)

For freelance projects we apply a transparent and fair fee structure:

| Item | Commission |
|---|---|
| **Freelancer commission** | **10%** |
| **Client commission** | **5%** |
| **Connects price** | **FREE** |

<Note>
  **Connects** (credits used to submit proposals) are free: you can apply to job offers at no extra cost.
</Note>

### How to upgrade to TRB Plus

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the sidebar">
    Log in to the platform. At the bottom of the sidebar you'll see your current plan (**YOUR STARTER PLAN**) with your cover letter and agent usage.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click «Upgrade to TRB Plus»">
    Click the **Upgrade to TRB Plus** button to open the plan comparison.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm with «Upgrade Now»">
    Review the included features and click **Upgrade Now** to activate your subscription.

</Steps>
