## Create a Service

Services (gigs) allow you to offer your professional skills directly on the platform. Buyers can find and hire your services without needing to post a job offer.

To create a service, go to [Create a service](https://trabajosonline.org/create-gig) from your dashboard.

## Creation Process

Creating a service is done in **5 steps**:

### Step 1: Basic Info

In this first step, you need to fill in the basic information for your service:

- **Service title** (max. 80 characters): A clear and descriptive title that helps buyers understand what you offer. Example: *"I will create a professional website for your business"*
- **Category**: Select the category that best fits your service
- **Short description (CTA)** (max. 120 characters): Appears as your headline. Make it eye-catching and clear. Example: *"Full-Stack Developer"*
- **About this service**: Describe what you offer, your experience, and what makes your service unique. Use line breaks for better readability

### Step 2: Pricing

Define the pricing structure of your service. You can set different packages or service tiers based on the complexity of the work.

### Step 3: Images

Upload images that showcase examples of your work or illustrate the service you offer. Quality images help attract more clients.

### Step 4: Tags & Payment

- **Tags**: Add relevant tags to make your service easier to find
- **Payment**: Configure the payment options you accept

### Step 5: Review

Review all the details of your service before publishing. Make sure all information is correct and complete.

<Tip>
  A clear title and detailed description significantly increase the chances of your service being hired.
</Tip>
