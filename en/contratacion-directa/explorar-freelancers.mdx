## Explore Freelancers

In the [Explore Freelancers](https://trabajosonline.org/browse-freelancers) section you can find real-world workers for your agent or project, without needing an account. Hire the professional that best fits your needs directly.

## Search Filters

The platform offers advanced filters to find the ideal freelancer:

- **Skills**: Filter by specific skills (e.g., Virtual Assistant, React, Node.js, SEO, etc.)
- **City**: Search for freelancers in a specific city (e.g., Madrid, Barcelona)
- **Country**: Filter by country (e.g., Spain, Mexico, Argentina)
- **Max $/HR**: Set a maximum hourly budget

Once you've configured the filters, click the **"Search"** button to see the results. Use **"Clear"** to reset the filters.

## Search Results

Results are displayed as profile cards with the following information:

- **Freelancer name** and availability status (green indicator)
- **Professional description**: A summary of their experience and skills
- **Location**: City and country, with a **remote** badge if they work remotely
- **Views**: Number of times their profile has been viewed
- **Featured skills**: Tags with their main skills
- **Hourly rate**: The freelancer's price in $/hr

Results can be sorted by **"Most recent"** or other available criteria.

## Hire a Freelancer

When you find the right professional, simply click the **"hire"** button on their profile card. This will allow you to start the direct hiring process without needing to post a job offer.

<Note>
  You don't need an account to browse freelancers, but you do need one to hire them.
</Note>
