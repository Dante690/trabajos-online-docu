# Getting Started

## Register a New Account :memo:

To interact with the trabajosonline platform, it is necessary to create an account. You can register using your email address or through a wallet.

There are two types of registrations: as a **User/Freelancer** or as a **Company**.
In this guide, we will refer to the “User/Freelancer” as **User** and the **Company** as **Client**.

## Registration as a User or Freelancer :bust_in_silhouette:

If we are looking for employment, either for a company as a salaried employee or as an independent professional or freelancer, we must register as a user on the application.
We can do this in two ways: with a wallet or with an email.

### Registration with an Email

To do this, on the page [trabajosonline.org](https://trabajosonline.org) click on the [**Sign Up**](https://trabajosonline.org/login) button and you will access the registration page.

<img src="/logo/registro0.png" alt="Email Registration on Homepage" title="Email Registration on Trabajosonline"  />

To register, please complete the corresponding fields with your email address :e-mail:, username :bust_in_silhouette:, and password :lock:. In this process, if you are registering as an individual user, make sure not to select the checkbox intended for company accounts :office:.

<img src="/logo/registro1.png" alt="Email Registration Form" title="Registration Form for User"  />

After completing the form, you will receive a verification email :incoming_envelope: in your inbox. If you do not find the email in your inbox, we recommend checking the spam folder :file_folder: and marking our email as safe.

<img src="/logo/registro6.png" alt="Check Spam Folder for Email Registration" title="Email Registration check Spam Folder"  />

Here we mark the email as safe ✅.

<img src="/logo/spam.png" alt="Mark Email as Safe" title="Email Registration mark email as safe"  />

Once the email is in your inbox, proceed to activate your account by clicking on the provided link "Verify Your Email" to activate our account :link:.

<img src="/logo/registro7.png" alt="Account Activation Email for Email Registration" title="Email Registration Activation Email"  />

### Registration with a Wallet

To register with a wallet, we must have the Metamask extension :fox_face: installed on the browser we are using (Mozilla Firefox :fire:, Chrome :globe_with_meridians:, etc.). Check that the extension is active in your **browser**.

<div 
  style={{
    backgroundColor: '#f7fff7', 
    borderLeft: '5px solid #2ca02c', 
    padding: '0.5rem 1rem', 
    marginBottom: '1rem'
  }}
>
  💡 <strong>Good to Know / Tip - Recommendation</strong><br />
  If you do not have a Wallet or want to know what a Cryptocurrency Wallet or Purse is and what you can do with it, you can visit <strong><a href="https://ethereum.org/es/wallets/" style={{ color: '#007bff' }}>What is a wallet</a></strong>
</div>

<img src="/logo/extensionactiva.png" alt="Check that Wallet Extension is Active" title="Extension for Wallet Registration on Trabajosonline"  />

To do this, on our page [app.trabajosonline.org](https://app.trabajosonline.org) click on **Connect Wallet** (if we want to register with a wallet). Then, choose a wallet from those available; in this case, we will opt for **Metamask** 🦊.

<img src="/logo/registro3.png" alt="Wallet Registration on the Homepage" title="Wallet Registration on Trabajosonline"  />

From there, the Metamask extension :fox_face: will open in the browser, where we select the wallet we want to use for the registration, which will also serve us to log in on the page :key:.
Once a wallet :purse: is selected, to verify that we are the owners of that wallet, we will be asked to sign a request with that wallet :pen:.

<img src="/logo/registro4.png" alt="Accept and Sign with Our Wallet" title="Accept and Sign with Our Wallet"  />

<blockquote>
  <strong style={{ color: '#00008B' }}>⚠️ This request will not initiate a blockchain transaction, nor will it incur any gas fees.</strong>
</blockquote>

This procedure is quite common and is also used by other cryptocurrency pages like OpenSea :ocean: and Binance :balance_scale:.

### Differences Between Wallet and Email

The fundamental distinction lies in the ability of cryptocurrency wallets to manage payments through decentralized finance (DeFi) platforms. With a crypto wallet :moneybag:, users can receive payments in various cryptocurrencies, taking advantage of the innovation and flexibility provided by DeFi solutions :chart_with_upwards_trend:.

|        | Payments     |              |
| ------ | ------------ | ------------ |
|        | Crypto Coins | Fiat         |
| Wallet | Yes          | Requires KYC |
| Email  | -            | Requires KYC |
